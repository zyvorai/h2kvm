h2kvm is dual-licensed:

- **AGPL-3.0** — open source. Free for home users and self-hosted deployments that accept the AGPL (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)).
- **h2kvm Commercial License** — for organizations that need more flexibility than AGPL allows, or a priced entitlement for a known number of VMs.

This document summarizes commercial options. Binding commercial terms are provided by Zyvor sales when you purchase.

## Why a commercial license

For organizations that require more flexibility, Zyvor offers an h2kvm Commercial License that provides:

- **Freedom from AGPL obligations** — Deploy and modify h2kvm without source code disclosure requirements
- **Proprietary integrations** — Build closed-source extensions and integrations
- **Commercial protections** — Warranties, indemnities, and legal assurances not provided under AGPL
- **Derivative work ownership** — Retain full ownership of your modifications and customizations
- **Priced by VM count, or a fixed Enterprise license** — Pay for N VMs, or buy Enterprise at a fixed price

## Commercial license options

| License type | Price | Support and updates | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Per VM | $100 per VM, one-time (N × $100) | No ongoing support | Converting a known number of VMs |
| Annual License | $25,000/year | All upgrades and Business Support included | Organizations requiring continuous updates and support |
| Monthly License | $2,500/month | All upgrades and Business Support included | Pilot projects and short-term deployments |

The Annual, Monthly, Major Version, and Minor Version licenses are **Enterprise**: a fixed price, not metered by VM count. The Per VM license is the alternative when you only need to convert N virtual machines.

## When you need a commercial license

You should consider a commercial license if you:

- Want to convert N VMs under a simple per-VM price instead of AGPL
- Want a fixed Enterprise license with support, regardless of VM count
- Want to build proprietary extensions or integrations without open-sourcing them
- Need to embed h2kvm in a commercial product without AGPL obligations
- Require enterprise-grade support, SLAs, and legal protections
- Plan to modify h2kvm without sharing your changes publicly
