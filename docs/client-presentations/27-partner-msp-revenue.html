5 Revenue Streams

Migration is the entry point. Managed services are the recurring revenue engine.

Stream 1: Migration Assessment — $5,000-25,000 per engagement

Connect to vCenter/Hyper-V/cloud read-only. Inventory all VMs. Classify by complexity. Deliver migration plan with timeline, risk assessment, and savings projection. POC: migrate 3-5 VMs live. Duration: 1-2 weeks. Margin: 80-90% (almost pure labor). Close rate: 70%+ convert to full migration.

Stream 2: Managed Migration — $25,000-250,000 per project

Full migration execution. Batch YAML automation. Windows VirtIO injection + Linux initramfs rebuild. Boot verification on every VM. Knowledge transfer + runbook delivery. Duration: 4-20 weeks. Margin: 60-75%. Scales with VM count: ~$500-1,000/VM for complex estates.

Stream 3: KVM/KubeVirt Managed Service — $5,000-25,000/month

Ongoing management of migrated infrastructure. Monitoring (Prometheus/Grafana), patching, backup, DR, capacity planning, incident response. Duration: 12-36 month contracts. Margin: 50-65%. This is your recurring revenue engine — grows as you add clients.

Stream 4: Training & Enablement — $3,000-15,000 per engagement

Train client teams on KVM/libvirt administration, KubeVirt/K8s operations, monitoring setup, backup procedures. Certification prep (RHCSA, CKA). Duration: 2-5 days. Margin: 85-90%. Creates client independence but builds relationship.

Stream 5: Hardware + Architecture Consulting — $10,000-50,000

Design KVM/KubeVirt target architecture. Server spec and procurement advisory. Network design (OVS, bridge, SR-IOV). Storage design (Ceph, local NVMe, Longhorn). HA/DR planning. Margin: 70-80%. Often bundled with migration project.
