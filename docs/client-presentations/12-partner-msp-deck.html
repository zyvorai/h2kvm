Engagement Model

Three service tiers for different client sizes.

Tier 1: Migration Assessment — $5,000-15,000

  • Connect to vCenter, inventory all VMs (read-only)
  • Classify VMs by complexity (simple/medium/complex)
  • Deliver migration plan with timeline and savings projection
  • POC: migrate 3-5 test VMs to demonstrate capability
  • Duration: 1-2 weeks | Margin: 80-90%

Tier 2: Managed Migration — $25,000-150,000

  • Full migration execution: VMware to KVM or KubeVirt
  • Batch automation with hyper2kvm YAML configs
  • Windows + Linux guest handling (VirtIO, fstab, initramfs)
  • Boot verification on every VM
  • Knowledge transfer + runbook delivery
  • Duration: 4-16 weeks | Margin: 60-75%

Tier 3: Managed KVM/K8s Service — $5,000-20,000/month

  • Ongoing management of KVM/KubeVirt infrastructure
  • Monitoring, patching, backup, disaster recovery
  • Capacity planning and optimization
  • Incident response and troubleshooting
  • Duration: 12-36 month contracts | Margin: 50-65%
