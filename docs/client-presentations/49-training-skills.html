VMware admins already know 80% of what they need for KVM — virtualization concepts, networking, storage, and troubleshooting are universal. This guide provides a structured 4-week learning path, concept mapping from VMware to KVM, certification options, and hands-on lab exercises. Your team can be productive on KVM in 2 weeks.

Total Learning Investment: 2-4 Weeks for Proficiency

A VMware admin with 3+ years experience can operate KVM productively within 2 weeks. Full proficiency (including Ceph, Pacemaker, and advanced tuning) takes 4 weeks. The underlying concepts — CPU scheduling, memory management, storage I/O, networking — are identical across both platforms.

Recommended Certification Path

Path 1: KVM Admin (Libvirt Focus)

  • Month 1: RHCSA (Linux fundamentals)
  • Month 3: RH318 (Red Hat Virtualization)
  • Month 6: RHCE (Ansible automation)
  • Total cost: ~$5,000 per person
  • Best for: traditional VM admin role

Path 2: KubeVirt Engineer (K8s Focus)

  • Month 1: RHCSA or LFCS (Linux fundamentals)
  • Month 3: CKA (Kubernetes administration)
  • Month 6: CKAD + KubeVirt hands-on
  • Total cost: ~$1,300 per person
  • Best for: converged container + VM platform
